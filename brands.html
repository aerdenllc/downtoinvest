Who I am

Mallory Warren, CFP®

I’m a CERTIFIED FINANCIAL PLANNER™ Professional with over 10 years of experience in finance who left a Wall Street career to make financial education accessible to real people. Through downtoinvest, I cover investing, ETFs, the stock market, and long-term wealth building — alongside the other side of money: how to spend it well. That means helping my audience identify everyday products that are genuinely worth the price, and encouraging intentional spending — putting money toward things that make life fuller, not just reflexively cutting everything. It’s finance content with real credentials, built for people who want to get it right.

Unlike most finance creators, the CFP® credential carries a trust premium with audiences. My followers know I know what I’m talking about — and that I only recommend things I actually believe in.
