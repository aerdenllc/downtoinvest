Buying funds isn't the same as building a portfolio.

Most people think they're diversified because they own more than one fund. Diversification isn't about how many funds you own. It's about what's inside them.

📲

You bought it because you saw it on TikTok, or someone in a finance thread mentioned it, and it seemed like a solid pick.

👥

You bought it because a friend said they owned it and it was doing well. It felt safer than picking something yourself.

📈

You bought it because it had the best performance over the last year or two. Past returns felt like a good signal.

🔒

The result: you own four or five funds that all hold the same 50 stocks underneath. They just have different names.

When the market drops, everything drops together. There's no cushion, no part of your portfolio holding steady, because it's all the same stocks just dressed up differently.
